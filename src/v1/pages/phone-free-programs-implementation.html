HOW IT WORKS

As guests or students enter the phone-free area, phones are secured in Yondr pouches.

Guests or students maintain possession of their phones at all times.

There are designated
phone-use areas located
throughout a venue or school.

When leaving guest and students tap their pouches on an unlocking base to open.
