Now that New Mexico schools have adopted their wireless communication device policies under SB11, the state has released $1 million in funding to help strengthen their implementation.

Funding can be used for eligible solutions, including the Yondr Program. Applications are open now through August 14 and are awarded first-come, first-served through OBAE. 

What the application asks for:

  • Basic school information

  • The items you want & amount, total funds requested, & a quote or budget backing that figure (we can supply)

  • Two short answers on why you need it and how it supports your policy (we can advise)

  • Your policy document and the PED guidance
