MAKE YOUR COURTHOUSE PHONE-FREE

Yondr provides the industry leading solution for cell phone security in courthouses around the world.

GET IN TOUCH

ENHANCED SECURITY

Prevents unauthorized recording and dissemination of sensitive courtroom proceedings.

MAINTAINING PRIVACY

Preserves the privacy of jurors, witnesses, and victims, reducing the risk of intimidation or harassment.

MINIMIZED DISTRACTIONS

Enhances the overall decorum and professionalism within the courtroom.

PROTECT CONFIDENTIALITY

Ensures that sensitive information remains secure and protected from unauthorized disclosure, particularly in high-profile or sensitive cases.

RESPECTFUL COURT ENVIRONMENT

Encourages respect for the court and its procedures by setting clear guidelines regarding cell phone usage.
