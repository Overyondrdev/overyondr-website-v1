GUEST EXPERIENCE RESOURCES

Yondr creates phone-free spaces, which may be new to your audiences. These resources ensure a smooth guest experience at your venue.

How Yondr Works:

A Phone-Free Experience

From arrival to final exit, Yondr makes phone-free shows effortless. Here’s how we create distraction-free experiences, every step of the way.

WATCH THE VIDEO

Fan Advisory Language

Fan advisory language communicates the phone policy to attendees so guests are prepared for Yondr upon arrival. This content can go on ticket sale and venue pages, as well as in Know Before You Go communications.

For best practices, we strongly encourage that this messaging be communicated to Guests on any ticket pages and the following schedule: 1 week prior to show, 3 days prior to show, & day of show.
