COMPLETE SOLUTION FOR STUDENT DIGITAL WELL-BEING

SCHEDULE A DEMO

Schools everywhere are working to help students develop a healthier relationship with their phones and other devices. The pairing of Yondr and Hackersjack provides a comprehensive approach that supports focus, learning and well-being. Yondr helps create distraction-free environments during the school day, while Hackersjack teaches students the digital literacy, safety and responsibility they need to navigate the online world. Together, they help schools build lasting habits for healthy technology use.

SCHEDULE A DEMO

YONDR PHONE-FREE SCHOOLS PROGRAM

Yondr helps schools create bell-to-bell phone-free learning environments while allowing students to keep their phones securely with them.

  • Eliminates classroom phone distraction

  • Improves engagement and attention

  • Reduces cyberbullying during school hours

  • Encourages face-to-face interaction

  • Strengthens school culture

HACKERSJACK DIGITAL LIFE PROGRAM

Hackersjack teaches students how to safely and responsibly navigate the digital world and understand the lifelong impact of their digital footprint.

  • Cyber safety and cybersecurity awareness

  • Digital citizenship and responsibility

  • Online well-being and digital hygiene

  • Real-world scenarios to which students can relate

  • Flexible lessons schools can implement easily

A Safer Digital Culture

  • Reduced conflict and cyberbullying

  • Greater awareness of digital risks

  • Clear expectations for phone use

THE POWER OF PARTNERSHIP

Better Learning Outcomes

  • Increased focus and engagement

  • More instructional time for teachers

  • Less disruption from devices

Future-Ready Students

  • Better understanding of the impacts of AI

  • Inspiration to consider cyber careers

  • Healthier lifelong technology habits
