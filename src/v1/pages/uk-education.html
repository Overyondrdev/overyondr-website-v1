PHONE-FREE LEARNING ACROSS THE UK

Yondr’s Phone-Free Programme supports secondary schools and trusts across the UK to implement consistent, whole-school phone-free environments that align with the latest government guidance.

By combining lockable phone pouches with implementation support, training and operational planning, the programme helps schools move from written policy to reliable daily practice.

HOW IT WORKS

What a day at a phone-free Yondr school looks like

ARRIVAL

Students place their phones in secure, durable Yondr pouches
as they arrive at school. 

DURING SCHOOL HOURS

The pouch locks securely. Students keep it with them, but phones remain inaccessible during lessons, break times
and transitions. 

END OF THE SCHOOL DAY

Students unlock their pouches using simple unlocking stations
before leaving school. 

The routine is simple, consistent and removes the need for constant phone enforcement by staff.
