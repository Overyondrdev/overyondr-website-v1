  • Yondr equipment does not require any power or electricity.

  • If your Yondr equipment was shipped to you, you can find the return shipping labels in the original box. Please place the labels on the original box and drop it off at your local FedEx. If you cannot locate the labels, please email your sales lead. If your equipment was dropped off by a staff member, your sales lead will be in touch regarding an in person pick up.

  • You can email your sales lead who will provide you with a return shipping label.

  • Yondr should be stationed before the metal detectors. This way any hidden devices can be detected that were not offered up to be pouched.

  • We do not provide metal detectors or any other security equipment. We only provide the equipment needed to lock and unlock phones in pouches.

  • Other than the equipment we provide, all you need is a table and chairs to be set up as close to the entrance of the phone free space as possible. No power or electricity is needed.

  • Pouches, unlocking bases and tote bags to hold the pouches. If you have a capacity of over 210, you will also receive hampers that the totes fit into.   • We suggest guests place their phones on vibrate so they can feel it when notified. They can then have their pouch unlocked to use outside of the phone free space.
